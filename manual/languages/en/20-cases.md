# Working with cases {#cases}

A case is one matter: a sale, a lease, a power of attorney. It keeps its people, the client's files and the documents you make together. Most of the work in a case is done by [the assistant](#assistant) at your request; this chapter shows what you see and check in Blankey.

## What follows what

1. [Make the case](#case-new) and [import the client's files](#case-files).
2. Blankey [reads known documents into profiles](#case-reading); you check the values.
3. [Give each profile its role](#case-profiles).
4. Ask the assistant to [make a template](#case-templates) from a document.
5. Ask the assistant to [fill it](#case-fill); you check the blanks and generate.
6. [Open, print or export](#case-documents) the documents.
7. Later: [fill again, collect missing data, delete the case](#case-later).

## Import the files {#case-files}

Drop the files on the case, or press **Import…**. Say what each file is and whose: the assistant finds files by these descriptions.

![Describing the files before the import](shot:cases-import)

## Reading into profiles {#case-reading}

With Blankey Online, known documents such as ID cards are read and the person becomes a profile of the case.

![While the files are read](shot:cases-reading)
![The ID card became a profile](shot:cases-read)

Always check what was read: the file's menu (the three dots), **Check values…**. A value that failed its check is left empty for you to type.

![Check values: click a field to see where it is in the document](shot:cases-check-values)

**Personal data…** in the same menu shows what is hidden from the assistant in a Word or PDF file.

![Select text to hide it, click a marked value to show it](shot:cases-personal-data)

## Profiles and roles {#case-profiles}

Roles such as seller or tenant tell a template whose data goes where. A profile made in a case exists only there: it is not on the Profiles page, other cases cannot use it, and it is deleted with the case.

A global profile is not tied to one case: it is on the Profiles page, any case can add it with **Add existing…**, and it stays when a case is deleted. That way a person or company you work with often is entered once. **Make global** in a profile's menu turns a case's profile into a global one.

![Role… in a profile's menu](shot:cases-role)
![The profiles of the case](shot:cases-profiles)

## Templates {#case-templates}

You do not build templates by hand. Ask [the assistant](#assistant) to make one from a document in the case.

![The templates of the case and the global ones](shot:cases-templates)

## Fill a template {#case-fill}

Filling is best left to the assistant: ask it to fill the template, and it picks the profiles and opens the fill form for you to check. You can also open the form yourself by double-clicking a template.

Roles are at the top, one row per blank on the left, the document on the right. Empty blanks are orange.

![The fill form](shot:cases-fill)

Whoever prepared the form, the check is yours: click a row's source to type a value or pick where it comes from. When nothing is empty, press **Save and Generate**.

![Choosing a source](shot:cases-fill-source)
![Nothing is empty](shot:cases-fill-ready)

## Later {#case-later}

**Fill sets** keep what you chose, to change a value or generate again. **Activity** lists what the assistant asked for.

![Fill sets](shot:cases-fill-sets)
![Activity](shot:cases-activity)

When data is missing, there is no need to copy it from emails and photos: with **Collection links** (needs Blankey Online) the person sends it themselves.

1. **New link**: give it a title, choose the profile the answers are saved into (or a new profile), tick the fields you ask for and how many files may be attached. You also set after how many days the link expires and how many times it can be filled in.
2. **Create link** copies the address. Send it to the person any way you like, such as email or chat.
3. They open it in their browser, fill in the form and attach the files. What they send is encrypted in their browser and can be opened only on your computer.
4. It shows at the link as **To review**. Open it, look at the values and files, leave out what you do not want and press **Save**: only then does the data go into the profile. **Discard** deletes it without saving anything.

![Collection links](shot:cases-links)
![New link](shot:cases-new-link)

**Delete case** removes the case with its files and documents. Profiles that exist only in it go too; global ones stay.

![Delete case](shot:cases-delete-case)
