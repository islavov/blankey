# The AI assistant {#assistant}

Blankey is meant to be used with an assistant: it reads the case's documents, makes the templates and prepares the fills, and you approve. Every case has an **AI assistant** tab with a chat for this. It needs a Blankey Online account that includes it. It knows the people, files and notes of the case, never the values in the profiles.

## Sign in to Blankey Online {#online}

Press **Sign in to Blankey Online** in the sidebar and finish in the browser, where you can also create the account. Besides the assistant, the account adds reading of documents into profiles, collection links and web assistants. Your profile values are not stored in it.

## Ask it

Pick a suggested question or type your own. While it works, a line says what it is doing and **Stop** ends the turn.

![A case with no conversation yet](shot:assistant-start)
![A turn in progress](shot:assistant-turn)

**Progress** above an answer opens the steps it took. Long conversations are summarised at the top.

![A conversation](shot:assistant-thread)

## What it asks you to approve

The assistant cannot type values or generate documents on its own. It opens a form in Blankey; **Cancel** refuses.

![Values for a profile: you type them, it learns only that they were saved](shot:assistant-request-profile)
![A collection link it drafted: check it and press Create link](shot:assistant-request-link)

![A fill it prepared: check each blank, then Save and Generate](shot:assistant-request-fill)

## Your own assistant instead

Without the built-in assistant, connect your own: any assistant connected to Blankey does the same work. **Copy link** on a case tells it which case to work in. Its requests wait on **Activity**.
