# Introduction

Blankey fills in documents from data that stays encrypted on your computer. It is made to be worked with through an AI assistant: you say what you need, the assistant prepares it, and you check and approve it in Blankey. The assistant sees the names of the fields, never the values. Some things can also be done by hand, without an assistant.
