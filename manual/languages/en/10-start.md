# Starting and the master password {#start}

1. Choose a master password and press **Create vault**. It encrypts everything in Blankey.
2. Save the recovery key, tick **I saved it somewhere safe** and press **Continue**.
3. Sign in to Blankey Online, or **Continue offline**. Either can be done later.

![1. The master password, twice](shot:workspace-create-vault)
![2. The recovery key](shot:workspace-recovery-key)

> The recovery key is the only way to get your data back if you forget the master password. It is shown only once. Keep it somewhere safe, outside this computer.

![3. Sign in to Blankey Online...](shot:workspace-welcome)
![...or connect an assistant on this computer](shot:workspace-connect-assistant)

Blankey then opens on Cases and offers a sample case to try things on. **Skip** starts empty.
